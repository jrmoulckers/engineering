# Engineering agent overlay

This repository's domain is software-engineering principles, technical mechanisms,
evidence, and justified reusable implementations.

- Read and follow ratified local principles under `principles/` once they exist.
- Treat the organization `.github` repository as the canonical source for agents,
  skills, and broad instructions; do not duplicate them here.
- Agents may propose or revise candidate principles. Only the repository owner may
  mark a principle Ratified.
- Preserve the Product -> Engineering -> Studio handoff: obligation and outcome ->
  technical mechanism and evidence -> user-facing expression.
- Do not introduce a package, registry contract, or Studio configuration without an
  accepted need and the appropriate architectural decision.
